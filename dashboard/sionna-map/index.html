How to shape it
  • Move a corner — drag its round handle. Teal handles are the building outline, amber ones belong to what you drew. Drop one on another corner and the two become one.
  • Remove a corner — double-click it. It asks first. Same for corners of anything you drew.
  • Delete a wall — double-click it. It turns red, then asks. Works on the outline and on anything you drew.
  • Set an exact length — click the measurement beside any wall, on the outline or on a line you drew, type a new value, then Apply. Reset puts the length back.
  • Draw your own walls — right-click to start, right-click again for each corner. The line follows your cursor. Right-click the first corner to close the shape and make it the outline.
  • Move around — drag the map to pan, scroll to zoom.
